Frequently asked questions

What is Galvus used for?

Galvus is a prescription medicine used with diet and exercise in adults with type 2 diabetes. Your doctor will decide if it is right for you.

How should I take my medicine?

Always take your medicine exactly as your doctor or pharmacist has told you. If you are unsure, ask them.

What should I do if I miss a dose?

Follow the instructions in your patient leaflet and speak to your doctor or pharmacist if you are not sure what to do.

Can I stop taking it when I feel better?

Do not stop or change your treatment without talking to your doctor first.

Where can I report side effects?

Talk to your doctor or pharmacist about any side effect. You can also report them through your national reporting system.
